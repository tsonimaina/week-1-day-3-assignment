About Vuta

Roadside towing, reimagined for Kenya

Vuta connects stranded drivers with verified towing operators across Kenya, the way ride-hailing apps connect riders with drivers. No more standing on the roadside making calls to numbers scribbled on old business cards — request a tow, see who's coming, and track them live.

We started Vuta after seeing how disorganized roadside assistance can be: unclear pricing, unverified operators, and long waits with no visibility. Our goal is to make towing as predictable and trustworthy as any other on-demand service.

How It Works

From breakdown to tow truck in minutes

Open the app, drop a pin on your exact location, and describe your vehicle issue. Vuta instantly matches you with the nearest available operator based on proximity, vehicle type, and current availability.

Once matched, you'll see your driver's live location and estimated time of arrival, along with an upfront price — so there are no surprises when the truck arrives. Payment is handled in-app once the tow is complete.

For Tow Operators

Grow your towing business with steady demand

Independent tow truck owners across Kenya can register their vehicle on Vuta and start receiving job requests in their area. We handle discovery and payments; you handle the road.

Every operator goes through a vetting process covering licensing, insurance, and vehicle condition, which helps build trust with customers and keeps standards consistent across the platform.

Pricing

Transparent, upfront pricing

Vuta calculates tow pricing based on distance, vehicle size, and time of day, and shows you the total cost before you confirm a request. There's no roadside haggling and no hidden call-out fees.

Operators receive a fair, consistent payout structure, with weekly settlements directly to their preferred mobile money or bank account.
